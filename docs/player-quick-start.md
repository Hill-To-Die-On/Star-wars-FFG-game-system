# Player quick start

The GM chooses campaign rule lines, available books and character ownership. Open the assigned character from the Actors directory. The system works without Director of Realms.

## Create or inspect a character

For a new character, choose Manual creation to make each choice directly, or Guide me for questions that narrow the choices. Review species, career, specialization, free ranks, resources, equipment and XP before applying. Ask the GM about any source-review warning. Ready for play completes creation; species and starting career are then locked through this workflow.

Existing characters can inspect characteristics, grouped or alphabetical skills, equipment, story resources and Advancement. The plus control adds custom skills. Click an eligible talent node to purchase it with XP. A purchased node may still require a manual decision: the Auto/choice/reference status describes implementation, not the full meaning of the book entry.

Configure Settings → Star Wars FFG → Help & rules coverage lists learned structured effects for accessible actors, with book/page references and source-comparison status.

## Make a check

1. Explain the intended action and wait for an agreed difficulty.
2. Click the skill or use the targeting card's Send dice to pool control.
3. Review the automatic pool and its reasons. Use Modify or Manual for an agreed situational adjustment; do not add the same modifier twice.
4. Roll only after the pool is agreed. Cancelling a preview is not a completed action.
5. Discuss success/failure separately from Advantage/Threat and Triumph/Despair. Use Spend narrative symbols to propose a costed outcome. The GM approves it.

A roll or approved narrative description is not proof that damage, a critical or a condition has been applied. Check the affected sheet or change history after the GM resolves it.

## Take a combat turn

Choose an available initiative slot on the correct side. Action/manoeuvre lights appear on the selected token and its sheet. Supported committed checks and movement update their allowances automatically. The plus control can buy a temporary manoeuvre when permitted, charging the displayed strain cost. GM policy may restrict manual additions and resets.

To operate a vehicle, use its Crew & Passengers tab or crew roster. The assigned pilot/gunner supplies the relevant skill. Double-click the portrait to open that character's sheet. Boarding, disembarking and role changes must be confirmed before relying on them in an attack.

## If something fails

Do not repeatedly click a purchase or Apply control after a timeout. Check the character, roster or history first, then ask the GM to inspect the request. A later undo may be refused if another change would be overwritten.

For a problem report, include the steps, expected outcome and system version. The Help window can download a small diagnostic JSON for inspection. Avoid attaching private sourcebooks, source keys, provider credentials or unreviewed campaign chat.

# Character creation and enemy guide

Players and GMs use the creation tools on a new character sheet to build a starting character from the campaign's database. GMs also have an **Enemy guide** for quick, original NPC presets. Neither tool needs Director of Realms or a paid AI service.

## Choose a creation mode

Open a new character sheet and choose one of two modes. Both use the same rules calculations and database, and both respect the GM's enabled rule lines and owned books.

- **Manual creation** starts at the catalogue, and you make each choice directly.
- **Guide me** asks about party role, approach to danger, natural aptitude and the Force. It then narrows the valid species, careers and specializations by their recorded statistics and skills. These are suggestions, not extra powers. **Show all allowed choices** widens a narrow match.

## Work through the steps

1. Choose a species, career and starting specialization.
2. Choose the exact free ranks the rules permit.
3. Choose starting resources and equipment. Equipment spending has a live budget check.
4. Spend starting XP, also with a live budget check:
   - characteristic increases use cumulative costs, with a creation cap of 5;
   - skills use career or non-career costs, with a creation cap of 2;
   - any XP you save remains available for talents and later advancement.
5. Check the final review, which shows the proposed result, and confirm.

Cancelling before you confirm leaves the actor unchanged. If the actor or the campaign changes while a draft is open, the draft becomes invalid rather than overwriting the newer work.

## Finish creation

Once the draft is applied, species and starting career are locked. The character stays in the creation phase while starting funds and any cited species exceptions are reviewed.

**Ready for play** checks that those tasks are done, then moves the character to the play phase. Direct characteristic purchases close at this point.

Starting a campaign does not finalise a new character who joins later. Older characters already marked as complete keep the previous campaign-start behaviour.

## Species exceptions

Species exceptions are not inferred. Apply the abilities from the cited source and use the sheet's source-review confirmation. The normal XP allocator will not spend past exceptional starting caps; those cases need an explicit GM ruling.

Owners and GMs can still edit the sheet freely, as usual in Foundry, but the system does not promise to validate those manual edits against the rules.

## Create an enemy

On a new, empty minion, rival or nemesis sheet, the GM sees **Enemy guide**.

1. Choose a role and a preferred combat approach.
2. Pick a species from the database.
3. Set the training level, or the member count for minions.
4. Review the preview and apply it.

The result is an original NPC preset with source-linked starting statistics. It is not a published adversary and not a player XP build. Minions receive trained group skills; other adversaries receive the chosen skill rank. Review species abilities, weapons, armour and talents before play.

The guide will not overwrite a linked minion combat group or an enemy it has already generated. For a published adversary, use the reference or private adversary import instead.

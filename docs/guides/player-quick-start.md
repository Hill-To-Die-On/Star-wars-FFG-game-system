# Player quick start

This guide covers what a player needs at the table: creating or opening a character, making checks, taking combat turns and crewing vehicles. The GM chooses the campaign's rule lines and books and assigns character ownership. Director of Realms is not required.

Open your assigned character from the **Actors** directory.

## Create a character

Choose **Manual creation** to make each choice yourself, or **Guide me** to answer questions that narrow the options. Before applying, review species, career, specialization, free ranks, resources, equipment and XP. Ask the GM about any source-review warning.

**Ready for play** completes creation. After that, species and starting career are locked in this workflow. See [Character creation and enemy guide](character-creation.md).

## Use your character sheet

The sheet shows characteristics, skills (grouped or alphabetical), equipment, story resources and **Advancement**.

- The plus control adds a custom skill.
- Click an eligible talent node to buy it with XP.
- A talent's **Auto**, **choice** or **reference** status describes how much of it the system implements, not the full meaning of the book entry. A purchased talent may still need a manual decision.

Configure Settings → Star Wars FFG → **Help & rules coverage** lists the structured effects your accessible characters have learned, with book and page references and whether each has been compared with the printed source.

## Make a check

1. Describe what you intend to do and wait for the GM to agree a difficulty.
2. Click the skill, or use **Send dice to pool** on the targeting card.
3. Review the automatic pool and the reasons given for it. Use **Modify** or **Manual** for an agreed situational adjustment, and don't add the same modifier twice.
4. Roll only once the pool is agreed. Cancelling a preview does not count as taking the action.
5. Discuss success or failure separately from Advantage, Threat, Triumph and Despair. Use **Spend narrative symbols** to propose a costed outcome; the GM approves it.

A roll, or an approved narrative description, does not by itself apply damage, a critical or a condition. After the GM resolves it, check the affected sheet or its change history.

## Take a combat turn

1. Choose an available initiative slot on your side.
2. Action and manoeuvre lights appear on your selected token and on your sheet. Supported checks and movement spend them automatically when committed.
3. Where the GM allows it, the plus control buys an extra manoeuvre for the strain cost shown. The GM's policy may restrict manual additions and resets.

See [Actions and manoeuvres](turn-indicators.md).

## Crew a vehicle

Use the vehicle's **Crew & Passengers** tab or its crew roster. The assigned pilot or gunner supplies the skill for the check. Double-click a portrait to open that character's sheet. Confirm boarding, disembarking and role changes before relying on them in an attack. See [Vehicle crew and passengers](vehicle-crew.md).

## If something goes wrong

- Don't keep clicking a purchase or **Apply** control after a timeout. Check the character, roster or history first, then ask the GM to inspect the request.
- An undo may be refused if it would overwrite a later change.
- To report a problem, include the steps you took, what you expected and the system version. The Help window can download a small diagnostic JSON file for you to check before sharing. Don't attach private sourcebooks, source keys, provider credentials or unreviewed campaign chat.

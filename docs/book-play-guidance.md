# Book-specific adventure and reward guidance

The creator-authorized reference catalogue already contains 421 adventure seeds across 17 books. Every seed has a source book and printed page; 78 have no description. The catalogue keeps similarly named seeds as separate source records. Director of Realms can ask `getAdventureSeeds({ query, book, page, pageSize })` for a GM-only, campaign-filtered page of seed ingredients. A seed is a starting point, not a prepared encounter or a source for unrecorded dice pools.

`getBookPlayGuidance({ book, kind })` provides GM-only, campaign-filtered preparation and reward prompts. The public registry currently holds eight original summaries checked against page images: Spy campaign guidance (*Cyphers & Masks*, p. 86), Mystic adventure guidance (*Unlimited Power*, p. 75), Seeker companion guidance (*Savage Spirits*, pp. 80–81), Spy XP review (*Cyphers & Masks*, p. 93), Spy non-XP rewards (*Cyphers & Masks*, pp. 94–95), Hired Gun rewards (*Dangerous Covenants*, pp. 89–91), Ace rewards (*Stay on Target*, pp. 92–93), and Seeker rewards (*Savage Spirits*, pp. 92–94). The exact book and printed page stay attached to each entry. `kind` can be `adventure-guidance` or `reward-guidance`.

Reward entries record decision points and an unknown numeric amount. They do not award XP, grant equipment, change Duty or commit any world state. For Spy missions, the checked section supports reviewing quiet successes, choosing session or mission XP timing while accounting for all sessions, and treating an exceptional mid-mission spend as a GM decision. The later pages discuss resource access, training responsibilities and gear choices. The Hired Gun section ties combat-heavy XP and pay to mission context, and distinguishes loaned gear from lasting rewards. The Ace section discusses occasional bonus XP and choosing suitable cash, gear, reputation, Duty or promotion rewards. The Seeker section considers companions or mounts, training, insight and standing alongside the ordinary XP baseline. The session wrap-up workflow remains open on the roadmap.

The adventure entries label each prompt as `prepare` or `during-play` so DoR can use them at the right point. They do not contain the book's adventure text, scenes, NPC statistics or a fixed plot. DoR should combine a selected entry with the actual party, the GM's campaign material and any privately imported adventure, then ask the GM to review encounter mechanics and transitions. The source book must be among the campaign's selected books when owned-book mode is active. Selecting no books returns no entries. The system adapter exposes these methods now; automatic Campaign Studio invocation and a live DoR adventure check remain pending.

## Scan coverage

A local, ignored section finder scanned the 66 newly filed PDF extractions for reward, adventure-running and seed headings or phrases. It recorded 78 **candidate pages** across 28 source scans: 46 reward matches, 18 adventure-guidance matches and 19 seed matches; a page can match more than one kind. Fourteen of the matched source scans currently map to registered catalogue books. The completed preferred-source text pass skipped 2,052 low-text pages. A local vision triage inspected all of them and flagged 569 possible adventure-guidance pages and 61 possible reward pages, with overlap and possible false positives. The finder writes `.local/rule-scan/book-play-section-candidates.json`; the vision pointers are in `.local/rule-scan/gap-page-index.jsonl`. These are search pointers, not verified instructions. PDF content and OCR remain private. Coverage must expand book by book, with each summary checked against the printed page before adding it to the public registry.

The separate LM Studio rules scan has completed its selected-source text and image triage. Its candidate instructions have not been promoted into this registry. Each book remains a distinct source, even when its advice resembles another book's.

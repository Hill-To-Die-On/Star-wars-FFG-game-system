# Combat and vehicles

These workflows describe the **unreleased gameplay candidate**. The system supports reviewed decisions; it does not silently resolve every rule in every sourcebook.

## Personal combat

1. Agree the intent and select an eligible initiative slot.
2. Select the attacker and target. Review range, sight, weapon and defence.
3. Use Send dice to pool, adjust situational dice and roll once.
4. Read success/failure separately from Advantage/Threat and Triumph/Despair.
5. Propose and approve narrative spending, then apply any mechanical changes through their reviewed workflow.

Range searches use legal edge-to-edge firing paths rather than only the centre line. Obstructions and supported firing arcs constrain the path. Ambiguous geometry requests a GM ruling. Range bands are an aid to the table, not a substitute for narrative positioning.

Combat resolution previews damage, soak, supported Pierce/Breach adjustments and recovery. Tabletop history supports conflict-safe undo. Critical and condition records do not imply that every associated modifier is automated. Reviewed narrative-option tables remain a separate source-coverage task.

## Vehicles and crew

Choose the model through the vehicle sheet's searchable field. The model supplies reviewed statistics and default artwork where available. Rename the ship freely. Incomplete profiles and unverified loadouts remain labelled.

Use Crew & Passengers to assign or generate reviewed crew. Drag a character onto a vehicle to board after confirmation. The compact onboard portrait opens crew management; double-click a crew portrait to open that character's sheet. Disembarking uses the management workflow.

Vehicle checks use the assigned crew member's characteristics, skills and supported talents. Review weapon, gunner, firing arc and defending zone before sending the pool. Four shield zones are distinct from dorsal/ventral weapon mounts. Vehicle movement does not automatically rotate the hull; use its rotation controls.

Native levels and elevation support vertical relationships where the scene geometry is well defined. Missing surfaces, overlapping levels and unsupported spatial cases still need adjudication. Cosmetic shadows do not establish line of sight.

## Minions and turns

Linked minions share group ranks, wounds and turn handling; defeated members leave the active connection and rejoin when restored. Check group membership before applying damage.

The GM chooses whether players may manually manage action/manoeuvre indicators. Automatic spending applies to supported committed checks and drags, not every possible movement API. Extra manoeuvres remain subject to cost and limits.

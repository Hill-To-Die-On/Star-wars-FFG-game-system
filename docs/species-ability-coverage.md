# Playable species ability coverage

The creator-authorized catalogue has 104 playable species. All 104 have a `Special` shorthand field, but that field is not a complete rule. Ninety-nine playable rows have a book and usable page. Arkanian, Drall, Gungan, Human - Corellian and Sathari have no usable source citation.

`data/species-abilities.json` currently records 39 species whose ability blocks were compared directly with held printed pages. The book and actual ability page are stored separately from the catalogue's section-start page; several entries differ by one or two pages. The registry contains original, short rule summaries and structured effects, not book prose or source images. Entries match by species **and** book **and** catalogue page, so a same-name species from another book cannot inherit the wrong rule.

New character creation grants the verified fixed skill ranks and asks for a required species skill choice. The usual rank-two creation cap includes the species grant. The roll builder and direct Actor rolls apply three unconditional check abilities: Umbaran Entrancing Gaze (one Boost die on three social skills), Balosar Antennapalps (two automatic Advantage on Vigilance), and Arcona Mood Readers (one automatic Advantage on Charm and Negotiation). Harch, Quermian and Xexto Additional Limbs each grant a second free maneuver in the native turn budget while retaining its two-maneuver cap. Roll and turn records name the ability and source page. Director of Realms receives the same verified ability register in character context; its direct skill rolls and turn budget use the Actor implementation.

The other 53 recorded abilities need a trigger, target, equipment, talent, movement or environmental state decision. They appear in the character sheet and reference catalogue with their printed source and **Apply when relevant** status. Character creation retains its species review marker until the GM or owner confirms the remaining effects. Existing characters are never silently rewritten: their sheet calls out starting skill grants that predate this registry. Klatooinian's second rank in a non-career skill remains a separate manual choice. The checked Ualaq source also corrects the public catalogue's mistaken Perception starting-rank sign.

The 65 playable species outside the registry have no verified ability effects in the module. Their catalogue shorthand is labelled as unreviewed on the sheet. The held-book scan can propose candidates, but each ability needs a printed-page comparison before it enters this registry. A campaign's owned-book filter governs which source species may be selected; existing characters keep their recorded source and abilities if the filter later changes.

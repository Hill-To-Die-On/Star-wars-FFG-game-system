# Character creation and enemy guide

Open a new character sheet and choose **Manual creation** or **Guide me**. Both use the same rules calculations and database, with the GM's enabled rule lines and owned books applied throughout. Manual mode starts at the catalogue. Guided mode asks about party role, approach to danger, natural aptitude and the Force, then narrows valid species, careers and specializations by their recorded statistics and skills. These are suggestions, not extra powers. **Show all allowed choices** explicitly broadens a narrow match.

The steps choose species, career, starting specialization, the exact permitted free ranks, resources and equipment, then starting XP. Equipment spending and XP have live budget checks. Characteristic increases use cumulative costs and a creation cap of 5; skills use career/non-career costs and a creation cap of 2. Saved XP remains available for talents and advancement. A final review shows the proposed result. Cancelling before confirmation leaves the actor unchanged. A changed actor or changed campaign invalidates an open draft instead of overwriting newer work.

After application, species and starting career lock. The character remains in creation while starting funds and cited species exceptions are reviewed. **Ready for play** verifies those tasks and changes the phase to play, closing direct characteristic purchases. Starting a campaign does not prematurely finalize a new character joining later. Existing legacy completed characters still follow the campaign-start behavior.

Species exceptions are not inferred. Apply abilities from the cited source and use the sheet's source-review confirmation. The ordinary XP allocator does not spend through exceptional starting caps; those cases still need explicit GM adjudication. Freeform sheet editing remains Foundry's normal owner/GM facility, not a promise that every manual edit has been rules-validated.

## Enemies

The GM sees **Enemy guide** on a new empty minion, rival or nemesis sheet. It asks for role, preferred combat approach, database species and training or minion count. The preview is an original NPC preset with source-linked starting statistics, not a published adversary and not a player XP build. Minions receive trained group skills; other adversaries receive the chosen skill rank. Review species abilities, weapons, armour and talents before play. The guide will not overwrite a linked minion combat group or an already-generated enemy.

For a published adversary, use the reference/private adversary import instead. Neither creation mode requires Director of Realms or a paid AI service.

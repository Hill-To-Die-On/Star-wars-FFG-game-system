# Community Code of Conduct

Star Wars FFG is an independent, volunteer-maintained project. Its community should be a safe and useful place for players, game masters, contributors, and integration authors.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when discussing defects, rules references, design choices, and review feedback.
- Welcome people with different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and access needs.
- Critique ideas and observable behaviour rather than people.
- Protect private campaign material, personal information, credentials, and unpublished security reports.
- Respect copyright and licensing boundaries. Cite book and page references without posting scans, copied rules prose, adventures, or official artwork.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, repeated personal attacks, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not accepted. Neither is pressuring contributors to share protected source material.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or accounts that violate it and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's contact details linked from the [Hill To Die On organization profile](https://github.com/Hill-To-Die-On). Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report. Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people needed to assess and resolve the report and will avoid conflicts of interest where possible.
